How to Find Us

Located in Mohalla Satar Pura

Rafaqat Clinic is situated in the vibrant neighbourhood of Mohalla Satar Pura, Kharian — in Gujrat District, Punjab. We are easily accessible from the main road and well-known to locals in the area.

Whether you are coming from Kharian city centre, the GT Road direction, or surrounding villages, our clinic is a short drive or walk away. Ample space is available for parking near the clinic entrance.
