About Rafaqat Clinic

Serving the community of Mohalla Satar Pura, Kharian with compassionate and professional medical care.

Our Story

A Clinic Built on Trust & Care

Rafaqat Clinic was established with a simple but powerful purpose: to bring reliable, professional medical care closer to the residents of Mohalla Satar Pura, Kharian. We believe that quality healthcare is a right, not a privilege.

From the very first day, our focus has been on building a clinic where patients feel genuinely cared for — a place where every consultation is thorough, every prescription is accurate, and every visit leaves you feeling looked after.

Our team of dedicated physicians — Dr. Faraz, Dr. Roha Anees, and Dr. Usman — brings a wealth of experience and a deep commitment to the community they serve.
