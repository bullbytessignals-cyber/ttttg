FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit?
No, Rafaqat Clinic operates on a walk-in basis. You are welcome to visit during our opening hours without any prior appointment.
What are the clinic's opening hours?
We are open Monday to Friday 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Emergency services are available 24 hours.
Are services affordable for all patients?
Yes. Rafaqat Clinic is committed to providing affordable care to the entire community. Consultation fees are kept reasonable and transparent.
Do you offer children's healthcare?
Yes. Our doctors are trained in paediatric care and routinely see infants, children, and teenagers for both routine and acute health concerns.
What should I bring to my appointment?
Please bring any previous medical records, test reports, or prescriptions you have. If this is a routine visit, no documents are strictly required.
