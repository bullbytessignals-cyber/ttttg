Dr. Roha Anees

Dr. Roha Anees is a highly regarded consultant physician specialising in internal medicine. She brings a meticulous, evidence-based approach to each consultation, ensuring patients receive the most accurate diagnoses and effective treatments available.

Dr. Anees is particularly experienced in women's health and the management of chronic conditions, including diabetes, hypertension, and thyroid disorders. Her compassionate bedside manner has made her one of the most sought-after physicians at the clinic.
